# Bandlink - User Stories for High School Band Communication

## Introduction

This document describes all the ways Bandlink helps high school bands communicate, organize, and coordinate activities. Each story is written from the perspective of real users - band directors, students, and parents - describing what they need to accomplish.

## Getting Started & Account Management

### Story 1: New Band Director Sets Up Band
**As a** band director starting at a new school  
**I want to** create a band organization in the app  
**So that** I can invite students and parents to join and start organizing activities

**What I need to do:**
- Enter my band's name (e.g., "Lincoln High School Marching Band")
- Add a description of the band
- Upload a band logo or photo
- Set up the current academic year
- Invite students and parents to join

---

### Story 2: Student Creates Account
**As a** high school student  
**I want to** create my account in the app  
**So that** I can see events, join groups, and communicate with my band

**What I need to do:**
- Sign up with my email address
- Enter my name and grade level
- Select my instrument(s)
- Join my band using an invite code or link
- Set up my profile with a photo

---

### Story 3: Parent Creates Account Linked to Student
**As a** parent of a band student  
**I want to** create an account linked to my child's account  
**So that** I can see their schedule, events, and communicate with band leaders

**What I need to do:**
- Sign up with my email address
- Enter my name and contact information
- Link my account to my child's student account
- Set notification preferences
- View my child's band activities

---

### Story 4: User Logs In
**As a** band member, parent, or director  
**I want to** log into the app with my email and password  
**So that** I can access all my band information

**What I need to do:**
- Enter my email address
- Enter my password
- Access my personalized dashboard

---

### Story 5: Parent Switches to View Child's Perspective
**As a** parent  
**I want to** switch my view to see exactly what my child sees  
**So that** I can understand their schedule and what they're experiencing

**What I need to do:**
- Click a button to "View as [Child's Name]"
- See the same events, groups, and messages my child sees
- Switch back to my parent view when needed

---

### Story 6: User Updates Profile Information
**As a** band member  
**I want to** update my profile information  
**So that** band leaders and other members have my current contact details

**What I need to do:**
- Edit my name, email, or phone number
- Update my instrument assignments
- Change my profile photo
- Update my bio or "about me" section

---

### Story 7: User Changes Password
**As a** user  
**I want to** change my password  
**So that** I can keep my account secure

**What I need to do:**
- Enter my current password
- Enter a new password
- Confirm the new password
- Save the changes

---

### Story 8: User Logs Out
**As a** user  
**I want to** log out of the app  
**So that** I can protect my account on shared devices

**What I need to do:**
- Click the logout button
- Confirm I want to log out
- Return to the login screen

## Band Organization & Membership

### Story 9: Band Director Views All Band Members
**As a** band director  
**I want to** see a complete list of all students and parents in my band  
**So that** I can manage membership and contact people when needed

**What I need to do:**
- View a roster of all band members
- See each student's name, grade, instrument, and contact information
- See which parents are linked to which students
- Search or filter the roster

---

### Story 10: Band Director Adds New Student
**As a** band director  
**I want to** add a new student to the band  
**So that** they can start participating in band activities

**What I need to do:**
- Enter the student's name and email
- Assign their grade level
- Assign their instrument(s)
- Send them an invitation to join
- Add their parent's contact information

---

### Story 11: Band Director Removes Student
**As a** band director  
**I want to** remove a student from the band  
**So that** I can keep the roster current when students leave

**What I need to do:**
- Find the student in the roster
- Remove them from the band
- Confirm the removal
- Optionally archive their information

---

### Story 12: Band Director Assigns Student Roles
**As a** band director  
**I want to** assign special roles to students  
**So that** I can organize leadership and responsibilities

**What I need to do:**
- Select a student
- Assign them a role (Section Leader, Drum Major, Equipment Manager, etc.)
- Set their permissions based on their role
- See all students with their assigned roles

---

### Story 13: Band Director Manages Academic Year
**As a** band director  
**I want to** manage the academic year and promote students  
**So that** I can keep grade levels current as students advance

**What I need to do:**
- Set the current academic year (e.g., 2024-2025)
- At the end of the year, promote all students to the next grade
- Handle graduating seniors (archive or remove)
- Start a new academic year with updated rosters

---

### Story 14: Band Director Views Band Statistics
**As a** band director  
**I want to** see statistics about my band  
**So that** I can understand participation and engagement

**What I need to do:**
